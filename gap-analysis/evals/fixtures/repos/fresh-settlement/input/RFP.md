# Contoso Rebate Platform: Request for Proposal (synthetic excerpt)

## 1 Purpose

Contoso wants one platform that accrues, settles and tracks customer rebates.

## 3.1 Settlement scope

At the close of each rebate period the platform shall settle every earned rebate.

- A settlement run covers up to **1,200 customer accounts** per period.
- Each approved rebate settlement is posted to the ERP as a **credit note**.
- Settlements above the Finance threshold are reviewed by Finance before posting.
- A customer receives a settlement statement once the credit note is posted.

## 3.2 Accrual scope

Accruals are calculated daily and are out of scope for this document.

## 5 Delivery expectations

The vendor shall deliver the platform in two releases. Release 1 covers accrual and settlement.
