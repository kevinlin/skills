# Gap Why It Matters Severity Workstream
CTR-01 Settlement run sizing contradicts the RFP
RFP §3.1 sizes a settlement run at 1,200 customer accounts; NFR-P02 allows 4 hours for 200 accounts, a 6× difference.		 The run window cannot be committed to until one volume is agreed. Critical WS6
G-01 Owner of the accrual reversal at settlement is unclear
Neither FR_M20 nor FR_M25 says whether the platform or the ERP reverses the accrual when a settlement posts.		 Both sides could book the reversal, or neither. High WS6
AMB-01 Credit memo vs credit note
FR_M24 says "credit memo"; RFP §3.1 says "credit note". The glossary lists no synonym.		 If these are two documents the posting logic differs. Medium WS6
